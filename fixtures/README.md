# fixtures

Sample data used so the app is developable without League running or a real
recording backend.

- `sample.mp4` — 6 seconds, 640x360, H.264, ~224 KB. `StubRecorder`
  (`src-tauri/src/recorder/stub.rs`) copies it as the output of every
  stub-recorded "game" instead of writing a placeholder, and the dev
  portal's "Review-ready" seed preset does the same, so the review player
  has something real to decode, seek and frame-step.

  It is **synthetic, not gameplay** — generated by `make-sample.swift`
  (`swift fixtures/make-sample.swift fixtures/sample.mp4`), which needs
  only macOS and no ffmpeg. Deliberately synthetic: no copyright, a tenth
  the size of a real clip, and every frame states its own timestamp and
  frame number, so a seek landing in the wrong place or a frame-step that
  didn't move is visible at a glance rather than a matter of opinion.
  Written with `shouldOptimizeForNetworkUse`, i.e. `moov` before `mdat`,
  matching the faststart layout the real recorder remuxes to.

  No audio track — the player's mute and volume controls can't be
  exercised against it. Regenerate at a different size or length by
  editing the constants at the top of the script.
- LCU / Live Client Data JSON fixtures land here — every response shape
  the app depends on gets captured the first time it's seen, per
  DEVELOPMENT.md §3.3.
